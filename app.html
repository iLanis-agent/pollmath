Poll Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page does arithmetic for a simple random sample, where everyone in the group had the same chance of being asked. It does not know how a real poll was run, so weighting, who did not answer, question wording and design effects are not in these numbers. It says nothing about whether any poll is good. The confidence levels are the fixed 90, 95 and 99 percent values and every figure is your own entry.

The margin truth

How far a poll result can sit from the whole group's answer.

The size truth

The smallest sample whose margin is no wider than the one you ask for.

The gap truth

The margin on the difference between two separate polls, each a simple random sample.
